Video Maker

A personal tool that turns a news article into a stick-figure explainer video (script, voice-over, illustrations, rendering) and helps publish it.

Who it is for

Video Maker is a private, single-user application run by its owner on their own computers. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.

Why it asks for Google Drive access

The owner uses Google Drive to keep the media the app produces (images, audio, rendered videos) in sync between their own computers. The app requests only the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it created itself. It cannot read, list or change any other file in your Drive.
