Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Video Maker is a personal, single-user application. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (scope drive.file): the app creates a VideoMaker folder and uploads, downloads, updates and trashes only the files it created there (images, audio, videos, subtitles). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your Google account email address, read once when connecting, to show which account is connected.

How the data is used and stored

  • Files are used only to keep the owner's own video projects in sync between the owner's computers.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored encrypted in the owner's private database. It is never shared.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can disconnect Google Drive inside the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files the app created stay in your Drive under VideoMaker until you delete them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Video Maker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
